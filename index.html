ICT Academy Mandate

Republic Act No. 12254 — E-Governance Act

Strengthening the ICT Academy for E-Governance

Section 27 of Republic Act No. 12254 strengthens the ICT Academy for E-Governance and directs the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) to reorganize and restructure the ICT Literacy and Competency Development Bureau for the operations of the ICT Academy.


The ICT Academy is mandated to serve as the National Center of Excellence for ICT Education; conduct E-Governance training; promote quality ICT education and globally competitive skills development; build partnerships; support continuous learning for educators; provide scholarship opportunities; undertake ICT research; and develop curricula and courses that strengthen ICT proficiency and competency.

ICT Professional Certification

National Examinations for ICT Specialists & Systems Analysts

Government Civil Service Eligibility

Under Civil Service Commission (CSC) Resolution No. 1200865, individuals passing the ILCDB ICT Proficiency Examinations (Hands-on & Diagnostic) are granted EDP Specialist Eligibility (EDPSE) for official IT positions across government agencies nationwide.

Application & Registration Process

Follow these simple steps to apply for the ICT Proficiency Examination (Diagnostic or Hands-On Programming / Systems Analysis).

1

Review Qualification Requirements

Ensure you meet the basic qualifications for the exam type. The hands-on exam evaluates proficiency in programming languages (C#, Java, Python, PHP) or Systems Analysis and Design.

  • Filipino Citizen, at least 18 years of age.
  • Graduate of IT/CS degree OR non-IT graduate with relevant IT work experience or training hours.
2

Prepare Supporting Documents

Gather soft copies or scanned originals of the following mandatory requirements:

  • Duly accomplished ICT Exam Application Form (Form 101).
  • Valid Government-issued ID (Passport, UMID, Driver's License, PhilID).
  • 2x2 Recent Passport Photo (White background with full name tag).
  • Transcript of Records (TOR) or Certificate of IT Training/Employment.
3

Online Slot Reservation & Document Submission

Choose an available examination slot from the schedule tab and complete the online registration form. Upload your required documents for pre-evaluation by the ILCDB Certification Committee.

4

Payment & Confirmation Ticket

Upon approval of submitted documents, pay the non-refundable examination fee (₱500 for Diagnostic / ₱1,000 for Hands-On) through authorized Landbank LinkBiz portal or DICT regional cashier. You will receive an official Examination Notice Ticket via email.

5

Take the Examination & Claim Certificate

Report to your assigned Digital Transformation Center (DTC) or testing facility on exam day with your confirmation ticket and valid ID. Results are published within 15 working days, after which passing examinees can claim their Official Certificate of Eligibility.
